Key Observations & Recommendations

Prioritised findings with recommended actions for remediation and rationalisation.

PriorityData Loss Prevention
No DLP policy is in enforcement mode
6 DLP policies exist but none are set to enforce.
Recommendation: Confirm which policies should move to enforce after a simulation/audit soak.
Needs improvementData Loss Prevention
DLP policies remain in test/simulation mode
5 of 6 DLP policies are in a test mode.
Recommendation: Decide per policy whether to promote to enforce or retire during rationalization.
Needs improvementRetention & Records
Retention labels are not mapped to a file plan
3 of 9 retention labels have no file-plan descriptors.
Recommendation: Align retention labels to a legally approved records schedule.
InformationalData Loss Prevention
Some DLP rules do not generate alerts
4 DLP rules have alerting disabled, weakening monitoring evidence.
Recommendation: Standardise alerting on medium/high-severity rules to support SOC 2 / ISO monitoring evidence.
InformationalClassification
Custom sensitive information types require review & validation
4 custom SITs discovered; their detection logic (regex/keywords, EDM, trainable classifiers) is tenant-specific.
Recommendation: Review and validate custom SITs; note that EDM schemas and trainable classifiers are not fully exportable and should be documented separately.
InformationalRetention & Records
Record-type labels are immutable
2 record/regulatory-record labels were found; these cannot be relabelled or deleted.
Recommendation: Plan explicit handling of immutable records.

Methodology & Scope

How this report was produced and what it does and does not cover.

This report was generated from a read-only discovery export of the tenant's Microsoft Purview configuration (Information Protection, Classification, Data Loss Prevention, Data Lifecycle and Records, and Audit configuration), collected via Security & Compliance and Exchange Online PowerShell. It reflects configuration observed at the time of discovery and is provided to support assessment and rationalisation. It is not a compliance attestation. Items that cannot be exported programmatically - custom trainable classifiers, disposition-review state, Compliance Manager evidence, and Activity Explorer trends - are captured manually and are out of scope of the automated sections above.
